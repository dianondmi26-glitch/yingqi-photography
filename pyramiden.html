A settlement does not disappear at the moment people leave. Buildings remain, objects remain, and the shape of ordinary life can continue long after that life has stopped.

In Pyramiden, abandonment is not an empty condition. It is a suspended form of occupation — a town no longer functioning as a town, yet still carrying the physical evidence of having once been one.
